What a rep sees

  • Their book, scoped: every adapter read is filtered by their Slack identity (already in the contract) — accounts, opps, activity they're allowed to see.
  • Team knowledge they can see: artifacts inherit visibility from origin — a #sdr-team thread is visible to #sdr-team; a Gmail thread belongs to its owner.
  • Tables + Knowledge base: read-only canonical truth, signals for visible accounts.
  • Logs: real executions (public to the channel), plus a private "My activity" view of their own runs.
  • Provenance everywhere: who built each play, who answered each cited thread.

What the owner (Ryan) additionally sees

  • Sources / connector health: every connector's owner, cadence, watermark, last run, failure state, rows emitted.
  • Review labeling: the docs-18 grading studio — labels double as the gate for what knowledge is kept and the source-gap queue.
  • Pending actions: the write-path approval queue (empty in MVP; RevOps-veto seam at hackweek; connector PRs awaiting review can surface here).
  • Audit trail: which evidence rows each run returned to whom (the evidence panel doubles as this).
  • Sim back office: engine admin, schedules, keys — reps never see raw Sim.
